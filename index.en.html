Everything you need to watch

Add your playlists, grab your remote — TiviEase Go handles the rest.

M3U & Xtream Codes

Add a playlist via URL, local file or Xtream account (server + username + password). Live TV, movies and series are separated automatically.

Automatic Audio & Subtitle Detection

Automatically finds your preferred language in multi-audio and subtitled content — the right audio track and subtitles are selected for every title, no settings trips needed.

EPG TV Guide

Program flow per channel with XMLTV sources. Automatic/manual mapping, time shifting and past-day support.

Recording & Reminders

Long-press a program in the EPG: schedule a recording or set a reminder. Get notified or let the channel open automatically.

Multi-Screen (PIP)

Watch up to 4 channels side by side. Equal view, big-screen mode and independent per-channel control.

Profiles & Parental Control

Everyone gets their own profile: separate favorites, history and settings. PIN lock, age rating and channel hiding keep kids safe.

Download & Offline

Download movies and episodes, watch without internet. Recording and download support to SMB network folders.

Phone as Remote

Turn your phone into a TV remote: D-pad, number keys, color buttons, keyboard — all in the mobile app.

DLNA & Cast

Cast to DLNA devices on your network or send content from your phone to the TV.

TMDB & OMDb Support

Posters, summaries, cast and IMDb ratings with your own free API keys. Fast loading with smart cache.

Start watching in 4 steps

Open the app

On first launch the welcome screen appears: pick the "Add Playlist" card.

Add your playlist

Enter a URL or file for M3U; server + username + password for Xtream.

Add EPG

Define your XMLTV address via Settings › TV Guide › "Add EPG Source"; channels are matched automatically.

Start watching

Select TV from the top menu, click a channel — full-screen playback begins.
